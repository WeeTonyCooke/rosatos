Privacy

Demonstration site, and not legal advice. What follows is a factual description of what this site actually does with data, written so it can be checked against the code. It has not been reviewed by a solicitor and should be before the site is used for real trade — GDPR applies here.

What this site collects directly

Nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising script and no cookie set by this site.

Third parties embedded in the page

These load in your browser and can see your IP address:

  • Google Fonts — serves the two typefaces. Loads on every page.
  • Google Maps — the map on the Visit section. Sets its own cookies.
  • ResDiary — the booking widget. Only loads once you open “Book a table”, and anything you type there goes to ResDiary under their privacy policy.

The pizza collection form

Online ordering is a demonstration and is not currently in use. Were it enabled, the form would send your name, phone number, chosen collection time and order to the venue through Netlify Forms, for the purpose of preparing that order.

Staff sign-in

/admin uses Netlify Identity, and is restricted to venue staff. It is excluded from search engines.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask what personal data the venue holds about you, ask for it to be corrected or erased, and complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie). Contact the venue at rosatosmoville@gmail.com.
