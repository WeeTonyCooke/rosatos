Terms

Demonstration site. Rosato’s does not currently take orders or payments online. These terms describe how this site behaves, not a live commercial service, and should be reviewed before the site is used for real trade.

Bookings

Table bookings are handled by ResDiary, a third party. Their terms apply to the booking itself. Bookings are for indoor dining; outdoor seating is walk-in only.

Menu and prices

Prices and dishes shown here are maintained by the venue and may change. The menu in the bar is the authoritative version. Allergen information is not listed online — please ask staff.

What’s on

Listings are published in advance and acts or times can change at short notice. Instagram carries the latest.
